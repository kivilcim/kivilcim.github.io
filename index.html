How I think / How I make

Good design starts
with better questions.

01

Understand first.

Start with the people, the context, and the friction. Frame the right problem before reaching for a solution.

Questions / Journeys / Opportunity
02

Make it tangible.

Turn possibilities into flows and prototypes. Explore broadly, then make the trade-offs visible.

Flows / Prototypes / Feedback
03

Refine with purpose.

Test the idea, question the details, and build a consistent system. Keep what helps. Remove what doesn’t.

Iteration / Systems / Handoff
A little about me

Istanbul to London, with one habit: make it clearer.

I grew up in Istanbul and moved to London in 2020.

Before that, I worked in ecommerce and media, two industries where people decide in seconds whether something works. That shaped my focus: understand what matters, give it structure, and make it feel natural to use.

The concepts above are independent work, not commissioned client projects.
